OCR Scope of Requirements

01

OCR Business Objectives

Introducing OCR at the disbursement stage addresses three objectives:

G1

Reduce manual data entry

Fields on disbursement documents are automatically extracted and populated into the CRA screens; CRA shifts from "typing data manually" to "visual check + confirm", reducing workload and keying errors.

G2

Improve verification quality & consistency

Extracted results automatically run cross-document checks and business rule validation with uniform rules and consistent standards; issues automatically register a Defect Code.

G3

Lay the foundation for process automation

Structured, trusted OCR data is the prerequisite for downstream process automation.

Positioning: OCR plays the role of field extraction / auto-fill only — it does not replace CRA review. The final decision to confirm, correct, or release any validation result always rests with CRA.

02

STP Eligibility Rules — Basis for Document Scoping

The OCR document list is not arbitrary: STP applies only to "clean cases" meeting the conditions below, and the document scope converges accordingly. All 9 rules must be met simultaneously, plus a 90-day validity check.

01

Individual

Corporate / partnership cases involve resolutions, authorized signatories, multiple guarantors — too much judgment for straight-through.

02

New car

Used / recond cars require VOC, payoff, inspection, AP import permits — too many variables.

03

E-Acceptance

Acceptance data and agreement date are generated in-system, with system-issued documents — no dependency on extracting and manually checking paper agreements.

04

Panel Dealer

Panel dealers are pre-vetted with standard deposit handling — no extra receipt verification required for non-panel scenarios.

05

Single beneficiary, matching dealer system

"Who receives the money" must be unambiguous; the fund flow is cross-confirmed by the system.

06

No payoff

No settlement of an existing loan — no settlement calculation, no coordination with a previous lender, no suspense handling.

07

Not FBT

Funding before ownership transfer is extra risk exposure requiring manual checks on dealer limits and indemnities.

08

No FD pledge

No manual verification of FD amount / pledge tenor / LOSO consistency against the approval.

09

No Floor Stock dealer

Wholesale-financing dealers are a high-risk category requiring extra exposure checks.

Relationship to the document list: the external documents required by STP clean cases form the core of the "OCR required" list in the next section. With E-Acceptance and Panel Dealer included as eligibility rules, documents specific to paper acceptance and non-panel / direct-seller scenarios (Seller's IC, Loan Suspense Receipt) are excluded from this OCR scope; conditional documents such as VOC (used cars) remain in the list to serve regular non-STP cases.

03

OCR Functional Scope

Defined along two dimensions: which documents go through OCR (by data source), and what OCR does with them (four actions).

3.1 Document scope — three categories by "where the data comes from"

Already captured

Collected and verified in earlier stages; data is reused — no repeat OCR.

System-generated

Generated by the system; fields already structured in the database — no OCR needed.

OCR required

External documents appearing for the first time at disbursement — this is the OCR scope.

3.2 What OCR does — four actions
  1. Auto classification
    Sales upload without pre-selecting file types; the system identifies document categories automatically (including splitting multiple documents within one PDF).
  2. Field extraction
    Structured data is extracted per the agreed field list for each document type, with confidence scores.
  3. Document check (OCR Workbench)
    Get the documents themselves right first: is everything there, do the documents agree with each other, and do the simple checks on dates / amounts / fixed fees add up; anything the machine is unsure about goes to a person to eyeball against the original.
  4. Result delivery
    Checked data is written to CDM for the CRA side to pick up (how it gets picked up and checked — next section).
04

How the Data Gets to CRA & How It Gets Checked

The key things to confirm today: how the data comes across, how the checking flows, and what the screen shows.

4.1 How the data syncs
  1. CRA picks up OCR data from CDM
    Once OCR has read and checked the documents, the data lands in CDM; the CRA side pulls it directly, and the disbursement team decides how it fills into the CRA screens.
4.2 Checking happens in two steps
① OCR WorkbenchCheck the documents: right type, fields read correctly
→
SubmitConfirm the OCR results look right
→
② CRA WorkbenchCheck the business: run business rules, confirm results
→
Results on main pageOne more click to confirm

In plain terms: sort out the documents first in the OCR Workbench, then head back to the CRA Workbench to run the business rules — one step for the documents, one for the business, each covering its own ground.

4.3 What the screen shows
  1. Loading spinner after submit
    After clicking submit, the page shows a spinner while the business checks run in the background.
  2. Results land on the main page
    When the checks come back, the results sit right on the main page — take a look, and one more click to confirm.
